Coding

HackerRank — Hackathon 2027

Class IX–XII · Online · 1 participant
+

Solve problems in Python 3 on HackerRank under a single online round. Accuracy, efficiency and fair play are scored directly; AI tools or shared solutions mean immediate disqualification.

Format

  • Single-round, entirely online competitive programming event.
  • Python 3 only — other languages or older versions aren't evaluated.
  • Participants need a registered, active HackerRank account beforehand.
  • Contest link shared on Discord 24 hours before the event.

Judged on

  • Test cases passed against predefined cases.
  • Efficiency — exceeding time/memory limits fails the case even if logic is correct.
  • Tie-breaker: fastest correct submission (including penalties) ranks higher.

Ascension — E-sports Tournament

Class IX–XII · Online · 4–5 participants
+

"Battle. Race. Outplay." An inter-school esports tournament across Valorant, Rocket League and BGMI — fully online.

Valorant

  • Team of 5. Swiftplay prelims, single elimination.
  • Semis and finals run as custom matches, knockout bracket.
  • Map selection before matches; stable internet required.

Rocket League & BGMI

  • Rocket League: team of 4, single-match knockout, overtime breaks ties.
  • BGMI: squad of 4, three matches in custom rooms, ranked by total points.
  • Hacks or unfair tools = disqualification across all titles.

Dynamic Racing Showdown — Obstacle Race

Class IX–XII · Online + Offline · 2 participants
+

"Build. Race. Own the track." Teams design and race a wireless chassis through an obstacle course, testing precision, power and control.

Build rules

  • Max weight 3 kg, dimensions 30×20×15 cm (L×W×H).
  • Must be wireless — no wired control permitted.
  • Prelim: submit a video demo explaining chassis functions and performance.

Final round (offline)

  • Three rounds: Round 1, semis, finals — lap times recorded each round.
  • Track-limit penalties: green +5s, yellow +7s, red +10s.
  • Only 2 members per team may enter the arena per round.
