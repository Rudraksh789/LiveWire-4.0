What runs here

Four tracks, twelve events.

Each event runs an online prelim followed, for most, by an offline final on campus — judged, timed, and unforgiving of plagiarism.

Coding

HackerRank · APPetite · Sandbox Mania

Competitive programming, app builds, and Scratch game design — for logic-first minds.

Gaming

Ascension · Dynamic Racing Showdown

An online esports bracket and an offline wireless-chassis obstacle race.

Designing

Paradox Palette · Aperture Alchemy · Frame by Frame · Sound Bytes

Posters, photography, and film — original work only, judged on craft and concept.
